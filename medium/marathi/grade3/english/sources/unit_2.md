My Friend, the Clock

a. Observe the clocks and tell where do you see them?

b. Listen and read.

When I was very small, I often looked at
the clock on the wall. It made a sound again
and again, “tick-tick, tick-tick.”
At that time, I thought the clock was only
making noise. I did not know that it was trying
to tell me something.

As I grew a little older, I began to notice
its numbers and hands. The big hand and the
small hand moved round and round. Slowly, I
understood that the clock had a special language.
Always it showed me the time. The clock became
my good friend.
Even now, it tells me when to get up in the
morning. It reminds me when to go to school.
It shows me the time to study and do my
homework. It shows me time to play with my
friends.
