International Tiger Day is celebrated on 29th July. Discuss
with peers.

a. Read carefully.

My body is orange with black stripes.
My eyes are bright, my teeth are sharp and
my legs are strong. I look beautiful and brave,
don’t I?
I live in the forest. I like places with tall
green trees, cool rivers and soft grass. My
home is called a den. I rest there during the
day. I hunt when I feel hungry. I can make a
loud roar. My roar can be heard in the forest
from far. It helps me talk to other tigers and
show that this is my home. I am a meat-eater.
I hunt deer and other animals. I move
quietly and slowly when I hunt.
This quiet walk is called a stalk. When
I see my prey, I can run very fast to catch it.
I can also swim well when I need to cross
a river.
Do you know what a baby tiger is
called? It’s called a cub! When I was a cub, my mother took care of me.

Money cannot Buy Everything

a. Read aloud and discuss.
Aman was eight years old. He was the only child of his parents. Aman’s
father was very busy. He came home after Aman had slept. He remained
asleep by the time Aman left for school. Aman wanted his father to be with
him.
On Saturday evening, Aman kept awake till his father came. He went to
him and asked, “Father how much do you earn in a day?”
Aman’s father was confused and asked, “My dear son, why are you
asking this question?”
Aman said, “Please tell me, Father. At least tell me the amount you earn
in an hour.”
Aman’s father replied, “Around Rs.500.” Listening to this, Aman
immediately ran to his room and came back with his piggy bank. He handed
it to his father and said, “I have saved around Rs. 1,500 in my piggy bank.
Can you spend just three hours with me? I want you to be with me, play
with me and have dinner with me.”
Aman’s father was lost for words and embraced his son.
