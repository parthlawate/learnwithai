One day, Rina Madam walked into Grade Three class with a small
white box in her hands. “Good morning, everyone!” she said with a smile.
“Good morning, Madam!” the learners greeted. Rina Madam held up
the box and said, “Can anyone guess what it is?” Aarav raised his hand and

Everyone laughed loudly. “No, Aarav,” said Rina Madam. “This
is a first aid box. It’s a special box used when someone gets injured”.
She picked up the box in her hands and told the learners how the first aid
box helps to cure small injuries. “Hence, we must keep this first aid box in
our school and at home too.”
