b. Listen, read and enact.

It was a summer day. The
sun was hot. The air was fresh
and clear. A grasshopper sat
on a leaf. He was happy. He
sang loudly. He jumped from
leaf to leaf. Not far away,
an ant was walking. The ant
was carrying a grain of rice.
The grain was heavy. The
ant was tired, but she did not
stop.

The grasshopper laughed.
“Hello, Ant! Why are you working so hard on such a sunny day? Come
and sing with me. Come and play with me!”
The ant said, “No, Grasshopper. I cannot play now. I must collect food.
Soon the rainy season will come. I need food for the rainy days.”
The grasshopper smiled and
replied.
“The rainy season is far away.
You are worrying too much.
Look at the sunshine! Let’s enjoy
today.”
The ant shook her head and
kept on working. Soon, the rainy
season came. The sky was full of
dark clouds and it rained heavily.
Pitter-patter, pitter-patter.

---

<!-- Book Page 56 -->

The ground was wet. The fields were full of water. The grasshopper felt
cold and hungry. He had no food. He had no dry place to stay. His wings
were wet. He could not jump and fly. He could not sing.
He thought of the ant. He went to the ant’s house and knocked at the
door. “Dear Ant,” said the grasshopper, “I am wet and hungry. Please give
me some food. Please help me.” The ant opened the door. “Grasshopper,
what did you do in summer?”
The grasshopper felt sorry
and answered. “I sang, I danced,
I played. I did not work.”
The ant said, “I worked
hard. I carried food every day.
Now I have enough food for
the rainy season. Come inside,
Grasshopper. You may share
my food. But remember this, in
summer, you must work. Then you will not suffer in the rain.”
The grasshopper nodded. “Yes, you are right. I will work when it is time
to work and play when it is time to play.”
The two friends shared the food together. The grasshopper was thankful,
and the ant was happy to help.

learn to help others from her.
