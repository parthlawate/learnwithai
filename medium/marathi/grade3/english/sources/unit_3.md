#### a. Look at the picture and join with its family.

#### b. Listen, read and enact.

It was a summer day. The sun was hot. The air was fresh and clear. A grasshopper sat on a leaf. He was happy. He sang loudly. He jumped from leaf to leaf. Not far away, an ant was walking. The ant was carrying a grain of rice. The grain was heavy. The ant was tired, but she did not stop.

The grasshopper laughed.  
“Hello, Ant! Why are you working so hard on such a sunny day? Come and sing with me. Come and play with me!”

The ant said, “No, Grasshopper. I cannot play now. I must collect food. Soon the rainy season will come. I need food for the rainy days.”

The grasshopper smiled and replied.  
“The rainy season is far away. You are worrying too much. Look at the sunshine! Let’s enjoy today.”

The ant shook her head and kept on working. Soon, the rainy season came. The sky was full of dark clouds and it rained heavily.  
Pitter-patter, pitter-patter.

The ground was wet. The fields were full of water. The grasshopper felt cold and hungry. He had no food. He had no dry place to stay. His wings were wet. He could not jump and fly. He could not sing.

He thought of the ant. He went to the ant’s house and knocked at the door. “Dear Ant,” said the grasshopper, “I am wet and hungry. Please give me some food. Please help me.” The ant opened the door. “Grasshopper, what did you do in summer?”

The grasshopper felt sorry and answered. “I sang, I danced, I played. I did not work.”

The ant said, “I worked hard. I carried food every day. Now I have enough food for the rainy season. Come inside, Grasshopper. You may share my food. But remember this, in summer, you must work. Then you will not suffer in the rain.”

The grasshopper nodded. “Yes, you are right. I will work when it is time to work and play when it is time to play.”

The two friends shared the food together. The grasshopper was thankful, and the ant was happy to help.

*(Pictures of Kalpana Chawla, Lata Mangeshkar, Saina Nehwal, and Maharani Tarabai / Rani Lakshmibai)*

#### b. Read aloud.

Tarabai Modak was a great social worker and teacher. She was born on 19th April 1892 in Maharashtra. She loved children very much. She wanted every child to go to school.

In those days, many children from villages did not go to school. Tarabai Modak worked hard for them. She started schools in villages.

Tarabai Modak started the first *balwadi* in Bordi village in Thane. She made learning easy and fun. She used toys, songs and games to teach children. She believed children learn best by playing. She also trained teachers to teach in fun ways.

She worked for tribal children in Maharashtra. She received the Padmabhushan award.

Tarabai Modak passed away on 31st August 1973. She is known as the Mother of Pre-school Education in India. We should learn to help others from her.
